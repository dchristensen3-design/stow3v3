Playing for a good cause

The Stow 3v3 Classic is free to attend, with proceeds supporting the Stow PTO and its work benefiting Center School and Hale Middle School students.

Can’t make it, or want to give ahead of time? You can donate to the Stow PTO online. Donations are also accepted at the door.

Is admission free?

Yes! The event is free and open to everyone.

Can kids participate?

Yes! We’ll have a couple of kids’ knockout games during the evening, with prizes for the winners.
